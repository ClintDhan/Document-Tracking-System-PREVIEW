## Key Features


QR Code Generation – Generate unique QR codes with tracking identifiers independently of document registration. These codes can later be assigned to incoming physical documents.

Document Registration – Register incoming physical documents by scanning an available QR code or manually entering its tracking identifier, linking the document to its assigned tracking record.

Document Tracking – Monitor document movements and view their current status.

Status Management – Track documents through different stages, including Received, Reviewed, Under Review, Released, and Returned.

User Management – Manage system users and their respective roles and permissions.

Centralized Records – Maintain organized document records for easier monitoring and retrieval.


## User Roles

Super Administrator – Manage system-wide settings and administrative access.

Administrator – Manage document records and oversee document transactions.

User – Access document tracking and perform authorized operations.

## System Workflow

1.QR Code Generation – The system generates a unique QR code that serves as an identifier for an incoming document.

2.Physical Document Reception – The physical document is received by the designated office.

3.Document Registration – Personnel associate the received physical document with its corresponding QR code through QR scanning or manual entry.

4.Document Processing – The document is forwarded to the appropriate office for review and processing.

5.Status Updates – Authorized personnel update the document's status as it moves through the workflow.

6.Document Tracking – Users can monitor the document's current status and track its movement across offices.
